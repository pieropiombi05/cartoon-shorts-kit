# Ricks Clips: where the stories come from (catalogue checked Nov 2026)

The channel has 149 shorts. Two eras:
- **Before Oct 2026: comedy comics.** The narrator voices other artists' webcomics (descriptions credit TheJenkinsComic,
  The RedDot: "I just do the voice"). Hits like "The Sword of Lies" (9.6 M) and "The Guards Puzzle" are those comics.
- **From 8 Oct 2026: the 3D animated story format** we study (about 28 videos in one month). These are mostly **real,
  well-documented anecdotes** dramatised with a hero, plus a few **invented high-concept parables** and thought experiments.

## What it means for ideas

- Real anecdotes win. They come from the kind of material that already circulates as "TIL" facts: Wikipedia
  (history of technology, unusual articles, scams, experiments, idioms), long-read magazine stories (Wired on the scratch
  cards), classic psychology and neuroscience studies, fables.
- Invented stories work when one physical object or rule is at the centre (a button, the furniture moved one inch) and
  there is a twist; the abstract one (a memory economy) flopped.
- Every winner has a person (or "you") doing something in a scene. A topic without a scene ("how X works") flops even
  when the science is good.
- The mix is wide: 8 families in one month, never the same arc twice in a row.

## What it says so far (6 videos, small sample)

- The breakouts are both **"Earth without / with no [something everyone knows]"**: a global premise that touches every
  viewer, a thing removed, and consequences that escalate. Stayed 78-84 %, average viewed 90-99 %.
- Everything else plateaued at the ~1-1.5 k test audience: stayed 47-63 %, average viewed 60-79 %.
  - History and war trivia stayed only 56-63 %.
  - A niche science what-if (a candle in space) had 62 % stayed but only 60 % viewed.
  - The survival "you" piece (quicksand) lost 53 % in the first seconds.
- Thresholds that separate the two groups: **stayed ≥ 75 %**, **average viewed ≥ 85 %**.
